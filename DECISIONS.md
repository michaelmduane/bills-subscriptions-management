### 2026-05-27 — PRD Structure and Scope Options

**Context:** Project kickoff preparation — needed to define what scope options to present to the team for Cycle 2.

**Decision:** Present three scope options at Monday kickoff:
- Option A: Hub + Knot Linking (visibility + consolidation only)
- Option B: Hub + Knot Linking + Management Actions (recommended)
- Option C: Hub + Knot + Management + Bill-Specific Auto-Reload (full vision)

**Rationale:** Option B balances shipping velocity with customer value. Option A risks the "read-only dashboard" problem. Option C is the full vision but may be too large for one cycle.

**Status:** Pending team alignment at kickoff
