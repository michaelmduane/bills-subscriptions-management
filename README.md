# Bills & Subscription Management

> Cash App Card · Cycle 2 (June 1 – Jul 10, 2026)

A bills and subscription management hub that helps Cash App Card customers consolidate, track, and manage all their recurring card-based payments — driving recurring GPV and primary bank behavior.
