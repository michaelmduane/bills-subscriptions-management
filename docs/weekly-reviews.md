# Weekly Execution Reviews

## Bills & Subscription Management — Cycle 2

**When:** Every Monday, 6 weeks (June 2 – July 7, 2026)
**Who:** Trevor Luong (PM), Megan Tong (Eng), Gustavo (Design), Michael Duane (DRI)
**Format:** 30 min. Status dropped in Slack by Monday morning. Meeting focuses on risks, decisions, and unblocking.

---

## How This Works

### Before Each Meeting (Monday morning)

The agent posts a status check at 6am ET in Slack, tagging each DRI:
- **@megantong** — Eng status
- **@trevorluong** — Product status
- **@gbalestraci** — Design status

Each person replies in the thread with:
1. 🟢 / 🟡 / 🔴 (green = on track, yellow = at risk, red = blocked)
2. One sentence: what moved since last week
3. One sentence: what's blocking or at risk

> Replies are captured automatically and synced here. When in doubt, show more risk, not less.

### During the Meeting

**Red projects** get a deep dive:
- What's the blocker?
- What's the impact?
- What decision is needed?

**Yellow projects** get a check:
- What's trending wrong?
- What are we doing about it?
- Where do you need help?

**Green projects** (2+ weeks green) get a challenge:
- Challenge confidence
- Look for hidden risk
- Demo (optional)

## Week 1 — June 2 (Cycle Kickoff)

### Kickoff Agenda

**1. Cycle Purpose & Operating Rhythm (5 min)**
- Remind team of execution review purpose and cadence
- Confirm weekly status process (Slack by Monday morning)

**2. Scope Decision (15 min)** 🔓 OPEN DECISION
- Present three scope options (see Cycle 2 Scope tab):
  - Option A: Hub + Knot Linking (visibility only)
  - Option B: Hub + Knot Linking + Management Actions ⭐ recommended
  - Option C: Hub + Knot + Management + Bill-Specific Auto-Reload
- Eng assessment: What's feasible in 6 weeks?
- **Decision needed:** Which option do we commit to?

**3. Staffing & Squad Formation (5 min)** 🔓 OPEN DECISION
- Confirm squad members (eng ICs, design support, data)
- Identify any gaps or shared resources
- Confirm Knot partnership point of contact
- **Decision needed:** Do we have the right people? Any asks?

**4. DRI Kickoff Presentation (5 min)**
- Goal (outcome, not output): Increase recurring card GPV per customer by consolidating bills onto Cash App Card
- Key risks (3 max):
  1. Cold start / empty state problem
  2. Knot integration timeline alignment
  3. Detection accuracy at launch
- Confidence: _/5 (to be filled live)

**5. Other Items to Align On**
- Detection strategy: Cash Card transaction patterns vs. Knot-powered vs. hybrid
- Empty state approach: What do customers see on day 1?
- Knot merchant coverage: What % of our customers' common billers are supported?
- Weekly cadence confirmation: Same time every Monday?
- Ensure everyone has access to the design file _(added by Michael, 2026-05-27)_
